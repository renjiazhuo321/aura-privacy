番茄钟专注计时器隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 26 日

本政策适用于番茄钟专注计时器（英文名称 Pomodoro Focus Timer，项目名称 AuraProject，包名 com.auroml.auraproject，下称“本应用”）。我们提供专注计时、计划、统计和 Aura Pro 订阅功能。

我们处理哪些信息

  • 设备本地数据：专注时长、轮次、休息时间、音效和通知等设置，以及专注统计保存在设备上，用于运行计时器和展示统计。本应用不要求注册账号。
  • 使用情况分析：本应用使用 Google Firebase Analytics。它可能收集应用实例标识符、设备与应用信息及自动记录的使用事件；我们还记录专注开始和完成（时长、轮次、严格模式状态）、订阅页面浏览、月度或年度方案选择，以及结账开始（方案及商品 ID）。这些数据用于了解功能使用情况和改进应用。Firebase 可能根据设备和配置处理广告标识符；我们不在这些自定义事件中发送计划标题或专注笔记。
  • 订阅信息：购买由 Google Play Billing 处理。我们通过 Google Play 查询商品、购买状态及权益，以显示价格并解锁 Aura Pro。付款信息由 Google Play 处理；本应用不接收银行卡号。
  • 用户主动导出：当你使用“导出统计”时，Android 分享界面会将导出内容交给你选定的应用或服务；后续处理受该接收方政策约束。

权限与后台运行

本应用可在你启用相应功能时请求通知权限，以显示计时进度和完成提醒；可请求“勿扰模式”访问权限，以便在严格专注模式中调整通知打扰状态；计时期间可使用前台服务保持计时和持续通知。关闭相关功能或撤销权限后，相应功能可能无法正常工作。

信息共享、存储与安全

Firebase Analytics 数据会传输给 Google 用于分析服务；订阅交易由 Google Play 处理。我们不出售你的个人信息。本地设置和统计保存在设备上；Android 系统备份设置可能将其中部分本地数据备份到你的 Google 账号。第三方服务的数据处理及保留规则请参阅 Firebase 隐私与安全说明和 Google 隐私政策。网络传输使用相关服务提供的安全连接。

保留、删除与选择

本地专注统计可在应用的“设置”中清空。卸载应用通常会删除设备上的应用数据；如启用了 Android 备份，备份副本可能按 Google 的规则保留。Firebase Analytics 数据按我们在 Google Analytics 中配置的保留期限及 Google 的处理规则保留。若需查询或请求删除与本应用相关的分析数据，请通过下方联系方式与我们联系。你可在设备设置中管理通知和勿扰模式权限，在 Google Play 中管理或取消订阅。

儿童、更新及联系方式

本应用并非专为儿童设计。政策更新时，我们会在本页面更新生效日期。对隐私或数据删除有疑问，请联系 AuraProject 开发者：ren214370423@gmail.com。

Pomodoro Focus Timer Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This policy applies to Pomodoro Focus Timer (also known as 番茄钟专注计时器; project name AuraProject; package com.auroml.auraproject, the “App”), which provides focus timers, plans, statistics, and Aura Pro subscriptions.

Information we process

  • Data on your device: Focus duration, cycles, breaks, sound and notification preferences, and focus statistics are stored on your device to operate the timer and display statistics. The App does not require an account.
  • Usage analytics: The App uses Google Firebase Analytics. It may collect app instance identifiers, device and app information, and automatically logged usage events. We also log focus starts and completions (duration, cycle count, strict mode status), subscription screen views, monthly or annual plan selections, and checkout starts (plan and product ID). We use these data to understand feature usage and improve the App. Depending on device and configuration, Firebase may process advertising identifiers. Our custom events do not send plan titles or focus notes.
  • Subscriptions: Google Play Billing handles purchases. We query Google Play for products, purchase status, and entitlements to display prices and unlock Aura Pro. Google Play handles payment details; the App does not receive your card number.
  • Exports you initiate: When you export statistics, the Android share sheet sends the exported content to the app or service you choose. That recipient's policy governs its subsequent handling.

Permissions and background operation

If you enable the relevant features, the App may request notification permission to show timer progress and completion reminders, and Do Not Disturb access to adjust interruptions during strict focus mode. While a timer runs, a foreground service may keep the timer and ongoing notification active. Revoking a permission or turning off a feature may limit that function.

Sharing, storage, and security

Firebase Analytics data are transmitted to Google for analytics; Google Play processes subscription transactions. We do not sell your personal information. Settings and statistics are stored on your device; Android system backup settings may back up some local app data to your Google account. See Firebase's privacy and security information and the Google Privacy Policy for those services' handling and retention practices. Network transfers use the secure connections provided by those services.

Retention, deletion, and choices

You can clear local focus statistics in the App's Settings. Uninstalling the App normally removes its data from your device; Android backup copies, if enabled, may remain under Google's rules. Firebase Analytics data are retained according to our Google Analytics retention settings and Google's processing rules. To ask about or request deletion of analytics data associated with the App, contact us below. You can manage notification and Do Not Disturb permissions in device settings and manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

Children, updates, and contact

The App is not specifically designed for children. If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page. For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the AuraProject developer at ren214370423@gmail.com.
