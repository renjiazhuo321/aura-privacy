Effective: October 26, 2026
Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy applies to the TapMeld Android app (com.autoflow.app). TapMeld creates, records, and runs screen tap and swipe scripts.

Local data

The app stores script names, point coordinates, gesture paths, action order, timing, loop settings, run history, and settings on your device. During recording, the floating touch layer receives touches and paths made on it, then replays the gesture. Scripts are not automatically uploaded and there is no cloud sync. If you export a script through the Android share sheet, your selected recipient handles it under its own policy.

Accessibility and overlays

After you enable the service and accept the in-app disclosure, Accessibility uses window change events to identify the foreground app package name so point picking can return to it. It performs gestures from scripts you start. The package name stays in process memory. The service does not read UI text or screen content or take screenshots. Overlays display points, the recording layer, and the floating script launcher. The recording layer receives touches made on that layer.

Purchase verification and service providers

When you buy or restore lifetime premium, Google Play handles payment and provides a purchase token to the app. The app sends that token to a Firebase Cloud Function in the United States, which verifies it with Google Play. Current code does not store tokens in a database. Firebase App Check and Play Integrity may process an installation identifier, device and app version information, integrity proofs, tokens, and network information. Cloud Functions may temporarily process IP addresses. See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. The current app has no advertising or analytics SDK.

Retention, deletion, and controls

Scripts and settings remain on the device until you delete scripts that can be deleted, clear app data, or uninstall the app. The app keeps the latest 200 run history entries and deletes older entries automatically. Android automatic backup is disabled. You can revoke Accessibility, overlay, and notification access in Android Settings; related features may then stop working. Clearing app data does not erase Google Play purchase records. If you email us, we use your address and the information you provide to handle your request and retain the correspondence as needed. Contact the email above for data inquiries or deletion requests.

Security and changes

Android app isolation protects local data, and purchase verification uses encrypted network connections. We update this page and its date when data practices change, with additional in-app notice where required.
